SYSTEM FAQ

Is Oasis completely free?

Yes. Oasis is built on open-source principles. We believe privacy and efficiency should be accessible to everyone, not a premium feature.

Does it support Chrome Extensions?

Partially. We are building a compatibility layer for Manifest V3 extensions, but Oasis comes with most essential tools (AdBlock, Notes, AI) built-in natively for better performance.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. Oasis operates on a "Zero-Telemetry" policy. Your history, bookmarks, and AI chats are stored locally on your device and never sent to our cloud.
